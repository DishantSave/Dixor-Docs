Remarks

Aadhaar is a 12-digit unique identity number issued by the Unique Identification Authority of India (UIDAI). It features:

  • Exactly 12 numeric digits.
  • The first digit cannot be 0 or 1.
  • The 12th digit is a cryptographic Verhoeff algorithm check digit calculated over the dihedral group D5.
  • Data privacy masking (e.g., XXXX XXXX 9012) for UIDAI/RBI compliance.
